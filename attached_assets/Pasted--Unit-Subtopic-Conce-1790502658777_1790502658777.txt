	I. Geometry is Everywhere	"Math, Art, History

	Symmetry, Pattern, Harmony	"Math, Art, Culture, History
- Symmetry and pattern create beauty and order.

Research: How did cultures use symmetry in art/architecture? (History, Art, Culture)."																				
	Geometry as a Universal Language	"Math, Art, Culture, History
- Shapes and patterns are used by all cultures.

Timeline: When did these symbols appear? (History)."	"Comparative Geometry: Study math behind global motifs (Math, Culture).
Research: How did patterns spread between cultures? (History, Culture, Art)."																				
	II. Early Geometry in Art & Architecture	"History, Art, Math, Engineering
- Early humans used shapes for art/buildings.

Research: Why did early people use circles/lines? (History, Art, Math)."																				
	Geometry in Ancient Architecture	"History, Art, Math, Engineering
- Shapes like pyramids and ziggurats have structural and symbolic meaning.
- Geometry is essential in building design."	"Block Pyramids: Build pyramids, count faces (Math, Engineering).

	III. Geometry in World Cultures	"Culture, History, Art, Math

	Islamic Art & Tessellations	"Math, Art, History, Culture

Research: Geometry’s role in Islamic architecture (History, Art, Culture)."																				
	Greek Geometry & Proportion	"Math, History, Art

Research: Euclid and the origins of geometry (History, Math)."																				
	Celtic art features intricate knots, spirals, and interlacing patterns.	"Knot Coloring: Color simple Celtic knot patterns (Art, Math).

	IIIa. Geometry informs ArtDrawing as Accumulation of Shapes & Art History Connections																								
	Drawing with Shapes	"- Students learn that all images can be broken down into simple shapes, progressing from basic recognition to constructing complex and 3D forms.
- Activities include building and drawing figures from shapes, collages, and reconstructing artworks.
- Inspired by artists and movements like Matisse, Klee, Cubism, and the Renaissance, connecting artistic techniques to historical and cultural contexts."	"Concepts: All pictures are made from simple shapes; recognizing and combining shapes.
Art History: Paul Klee (“Cat and Bird”), Henri Matisse (“The Snail”), cave paintings, folk art.
Activities: Build animals/people from cut-out shapes; Matisse-inspired collage; Klee shape painting."	"Concepts: Break down complex images into shapes; layering; sketching as construction.
Art History: Picasso’s Cubism, Leonardo da Vinci’s sketchbooks.
Activities: Draw robots/animals from shapes; deconstruct/reconstruct a famous painting using shapes."	"Concepts: Deconstruct complex forms; 3D forms (cubes, cylinders, cones).
Art History: Renaissance artists’ studies, Albrecht Dürer.
Activities: Draw figures/objects using shape mapping and 3D forms."																				
	Proportion & Grids	"- Students develop an understanding of proportion by comparing sizes and using grids to copy and scale images.
- Activities range from simple grid copying to drawing with advanced perspective and proportional studies.
- Lessons reference historical uses of proportion, such as Egyptian art and Renaissance grid and perspective techniques."	"Concepts: Basic proportion through comparison of sizes; simple grids for copying.
Art History: Ancient Egyptian art (proportion grids).
Activities: Copy simple images using a grid; Egyptian-style portrait."	"Concepts: Use grids to scale/copy art; introduction to proportion.
Art History: Renaissance grid techniques, Egyptian art.
Activities: Copy artwork using grid; create proportional figures."	"Concepts: Proportion, perspective, and advanced grid use.
Art History: Renaissance perspective, Da Vinci, Raphael.
Activities: Draw buildings/rooms in perspective; advanced proportional studies."																				
	Geometry & Pattern in Art	"- Students explore shapes, patterns, symmetry, and geometric concepts like tessellations and the Golden Ratio in art.
- Activities include creating patterns, symmetrical designs, tessellations, and art based on mathematical principles.
- Lessons draw from diverse cultural and historical sources, such as folk art, Islamic tiles, Escher, and Op Art."	"Concepts: Shapes and patterns in the world; symmetry.
Art History: Folk art, Native American pottery, African masks, Aboriginal dot paintings.
Activities: Draw objects using shapes; pattern art from cultures."	"Concepts: Tessellations, patterns, and symmetry.
Art History: Islamic tile art, Escher’s tessellations, Greek meanders.
Activities: Design tessellated patterns; create symmetrical designs inspired by tiles."	"Concepts: Golden Ratio, Fibonacci sequence, coordinate graphing, transformations.
Art History: Parthenon, Da Vinci’s “Vitruvian Man,” Op Art, Vasarely, Riley.
Activities: Draw golden spirals/Fibonacci art; create Op Art; geometric transformations."																				
	Project-Based Learning	"- Students synthesize skills by working on collaborative or independent projects that integrate shapes, patterns, proportion, and art history.
- Activities include class murals, creating art in historical styles, and advanced interdisciplinary projects.
- Emphasis on research, presentation, and connecting art to math, history, and culture."	"Concepts: Synthesize shapes and patterns in a collaborative artwork.
Art History: Matisse, Klee, folk artists.
Activities: Class mural using shapes and patterns."	"Concepts: Analyze and create art in historical styles using geometric principles.
Art History: Choice of artist/culture (Cubism, Egyptian, Islamic, etc.).
Activities: Create art inspired by a chosen style; present findings."	"Concepts: Integrate art, math, and history in advanced projects.
Art History: Student-selected research.
Activities: Create and present complex artwork blending historical and mathematical ideas."																				
	IV. Geometry in Nature	"Science, Math, Art
- Geometry appears in plants, animals, and natural phenomena.
- Patterns like spirals, hexagons, and symmetry are everywhere in nature."	"Nature Walk: Find shapes in leaves, flowers, shells (Science, Math).

	Fractals & Self-Similarity	"Math, Science, Art
- Fractals are shapes that repeat at every scale.

Short reading: What is a fractal? (Math, Science)."	"Fractal Math: Calculate simple fractal patterns (Math, Science).
Research: Fractals in African village design, textiles, and nature (Math, Science, Culture)."																				
	V. Geometry in Modern Art & Architecture	"Art, Math, History, Engineering

Short reading: Modern art and geometry (Art, History)."	"Abstract Geometry: Create geometric art inspired by modern artists (Art, Math).
Research: How do architects use geometry today? (Engineering, Math, Art, History)."																				
	Geometry in Technology & Design	"Engineering, Math, Art, Science
- Geometry is essential in design, technology, and engineering.
- Coding, robotics, and design use geometric thinking."	"Shape Robots: Build robots from shapes (Engineering, Math, Art).
Show images of bridges, cars, robots: “What shapes make them strong?” (Engineering, Science, Math)."	"Bridge Building Challenge: Build a bridge from paper or straws; test strength (Engineering, Math, Science).
Short reading: Geometry in inventions (Engineering, Math, Art)."	"Design Project: Code or design a geometric pattern or structure (Engineering, Math, Art, Technology).
Research: Geometry in video games, robotics, and architecture (Engineering, Math, Art, Science)."																				
	VI. Geometry as Communication	"Math, Art, History, Culture
